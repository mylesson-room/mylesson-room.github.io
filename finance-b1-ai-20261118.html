02 · Vocabulary

Money words that matter

Learn six useful expressions. Then use them to talk about your own experience.

savings

money you keep for the future

I put 10% of my salary into savings.
fraud

a crime in which someone tricks you to take your money

The bank stopped a fraud attempt.
investment

money used to try to earn more money

Every investment has a risk.
loan

money you borrow and usually pay back with interest

She applied for a car loan.
risk

the possibility of something bad happening

AI can reduce some risks but create others.
reliable

someone or something you can trust

Is an AI chatbot a reliable financial adviser?

03 · Reading

Your bank has a new colleague. It isn't human.

Many banks now use artificial intelligence to study payments, find unusual activity and answer simple questions. For example, an AI system can notice a payment that looks different from a customer's normal spending. It can then warn the bank or ask the customer to check it.

AI can also help people understand their spending and plan their savings. However, the technology can make mistakes. An AI system might block a normal payment or give advice that does not fit someone's situation. A bank still needs people to explain important decisions and solve problems.

In 2026, financial regulators continued to study both the benefits and the risks of AI in banking. Their main question is not simply “Can AI do it?” but “How can people use AI safely?”

05 · Grammar in action

If AI makes a mistake, what will happen?

First Conditional

If + present simple, will + base verb.

If the bank detects fraud, it will send an alert.

Use it for a real possible future result. Avoid “If AI will make…” in the if-clause.
